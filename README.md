# Mars Pathfinder

**AI-Powered Mars Exploration, Mission Intelligence & Crew Rescue Platform**

Mars Pathfinder is an intelligent planetary exploration platform designed to support Mars mission planning, scientific analysis, environmental risk assessment, astronaut safety, and emergency rescue operations.

The platform combines NASA planetary datasets, geospatial visualization, terrain analysis, simulation, machine learning, and AI-powered decision support.

## Project Vision

To develop an integrated Mars mission intelligence system that helps mission teams understand the Martian environment, plan exploration activities, monitor crew safety, predict hazards, and make informed operational decisions.

## Seven Development Phases

| Phase | Module | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Live Data Foundation & Mission Intelligence | NASA data integration, scientific datasets, provenance and data intelligence |
| 2 | Crew Management & Emergency Rescue | Crew tracking, telemetry, separation detection and rescue planning |
| 3 | Hazard Simulation & Environmental Risk Intelligence | Mars environmental hazards, terrain risks and emergency simulations |
| 4 | Machine Learning & Predictive Analytics | Terrain classification, environmental prediction and anomaly detection |
| 5 | AI Mission Assistant | Context-aware mission intelligence and decision support |
| 6 | Mars Digital Twin | Interactive mission simulation, timeline and scenario replay |
| 7 | ICARUS – Personal AI Voice Assistant | Personalized voice interaction, individual memory and controlled automation |

## Current Development Status

Phases 1–3: Under implementation and validation.

Phases 4–7: Planned development.

Implementation status will be updated as each phase is completed and tested.

## Technology Stack

- Frontend: React, TypeScript, Vite
- 3D Planetary Visualization: CesiumJS
- 2D Mapping: OpenLayers
- Backend: Go, Fiber
- Scientific Processing: Python
- Database: PostgreSQL, PostGIS
- Infrastructure: Docker, Nginx

## NASA Data and Scientific Resources

The project uses publicly available planetary science resources, including relevant datasets and services from NASA's Planetary Data System and Mars exploration missions.

Data availability, observation dates, provenance, resolution and scientific limitations must be considered when interpreting results.

## Project Goals

- Explore Mars using planetary scientific datasets.
- Analyze terrain and environmental conditions.
- Support mission planning and crew safety.
- Simulate emergency scenarios and rescue operations.
- Develop predictive models for planetary exploration.
- Provide explainable AI-assisted mission decisions.
- Build an interactive Mars mission digital twin.
- Introduce personalized voice-based mission assistance.

## Important Notice

Mars Pathfinder is a research and development project. Simulated telemetry, environmental estimates, predictions and mission recommendations must not be interpreted as live astronaut monitoring, certified safety systems or official NASA operational guidance.

All data sources and model outputs should be identified by their provenance and limitations.

## Security

Security validation is a mandatory requirement before production deployment. Critical and high-severity security findings must be remediated or formally accepted before production release.
